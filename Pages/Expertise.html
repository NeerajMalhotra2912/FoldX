EXPERTISE

At FOLDX Canopy Solutions, we harness the power of cutting-edge technology and industry-leading practices to design and manufacture a comprehensive range of high-performance canopies. Our expertise spans various types of canopy solutions, including Framed canopies, Tensile structures, and Fully customized designs that perfectly balance functionality with visual appeal.

We are particularly renowned for our Passenger Boarding Bridges (Aero bridges), which are instrumental in enhancing the efficiency and safety of airport operations. Our deep experience in this specialized field has empowered us to create innovative canopy solutions across diverse applications, setting new industry standards.

With a relentless focus on quality, precision, and customer satisfaction, we are not only advancing the manufacturing of new canopies but also providing exceptional service for existing structures. At FOLDX, we are dedicated to shaping the future of canopy solutions, one innovative design at a time.
